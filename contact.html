Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding permits, flights, safety, and booking.

Yes, Gilgit-Baltistan is recognized as one of the safest and most hospitable regions in South Asia, with nearly zero violent crime against travelers. Female travelers consistently report feeling welcomed, safe, and deeply respected by the local communities of Hunza, Skardu, and Astore.

Direct commercial flights operate daily from Islamabad to Skardu and Gilgit (approx. 45–60 minutes), offering spectacular aerial views of Nanga Parbat and K2. Mountain flights are weather-dependent; if cancelled, our standby 4WD fleet drives via the scenic Karakoram Highway without losing travel days.

Most general tourist routes (Hunza, Skardu city, Katpana Desert, Attabad Lake) require only a standard Pakistan Tourist E-Visa. For restricted high-altitude border zones or mountaineering above 6,500m (like K2 Gondogoro La), an official Ministry of Tourism Trekking Permit is mandatory, which VOYA arranges entirely on your behalf.

Safety is paramount. If extreme weather temporarily closes high passes like Deosai or Babusar Top, our local coordinators immediately pivot your itinerary to alternative sunlit valleys (such as lower Hunza, Shigar Valley, or Khaplu) with zero penalty fees.
