Expeditions & Mountain Services

Engineered for adventure, tailored for safety. We provide fully equipped logistical support for traversing the high wilderness of Northern Pakistan.

What We Deliver

Comprehensive Expedition Logistics

Whether you seek a rugged wilderness crossing or refined mountain hospitality, every itinerary is custom-crafted.

🚙

4x4 Off-Road Jeep Safaris

Tackle the famous cliff-hugging mountain tracks of Fairy Meadows, Deosai Plains, and Shimshal Valley. Our fleet of upgraded Toyota Prado and Land Cruisers includes licensed mountain drivers and satellite radios.

  • • Skilled high-pass drivers
  • • Off-road recovery equipment
  • • Fuel & maintenance contingencies
🧗‍♀️

High-Altitude Trekking & Basecamps

Guided ascents to iconic basecamps: Nanga Parbat North & South Faces, K2 Basecamp via Baltoro Glacier, and Rakaposhi Viewpoint. We handle porters, gear transfers, and high-altitude mountain first-aid.

  • • Certified Balti & Hunzakut guides
  • • Pulse oximeters & medical oxygen
  • • High-altitude nutrition & mess tents
⛺

Luxury Glamping & Stargazing Domes

Fall asleep to the sound of whispering winds under the Bortle Class 1 dark skies of Katpana Desert and Deosai Plateau. Insulated geodesic domes with plush heated bedding, en-suite washrooms, and gourmet local dining.

  • • Climate-controlled dome tents
  • • High-powered telescope sessions
  • • Traditional campfire musical nights
🏰

Silk Road & Fort Heritage Tours

Immerse yourself in centuries of Tibetan and Central Asian influence. Exclusive guided tours of 700-year-old Baltit & Altit Forts, Shigar Fort Royal Residence, and ancient Buddhist rock carvings in Manthal.

  • • Royal palace historical curators
  • • Organic apricot orchard visits
  • • Traditional Wakhi musical heritage
🚁

Helicopter Safaris & Aerial Flights

Witness the majesty of 4 of the world's 14 eight-thousanders in a single breathtaking flight. Aerial panoramas of K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum, and the confluence of the Indus and Gilgit Rivers.

  • • Private VIP charter arrangements
  • • Emergency aerial evacuation backup
  • • Aerial photography flight paths
📸

Photography & Drone Expeditions

Specialized routes timed for golden hour light across the Karakoram Highway. We assist with drone flight clearances, charging stations in remote valleys, and scouting the finest vantage points.

  • • Government drone permit clearance
  • • Autumn blossom & foliage timing
  • • Astro-landscape shooting workshops
